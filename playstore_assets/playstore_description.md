# Google Play Store Listing Documentation

This document contains the metadata, description, and asset checklist required to publish the **RayGlides Driver** app on the Google Play Store.

---

## 1. App Metadata

- **App Name (Title)**: `RayGlides Driver` (Max 30 characters)
- **Short Description**: `Track solar charging, monitor battery health, and measure daily fuel cost savings of your RayGlides EV kit.` (Max 80 characters)
- **Category**: `Auto & Vehicles` / `Maps & Navigation`
- **Content Rating**: `Everyone (PEGI 3)`

---

## 2. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

```text
Optimize your electric loader vehicle with RayGlides Driver—the ultimate companion app for RayGlides Smart Solar Conversion Kits! 

Built specifically for 3-wheeler and 4-wheeler electric commercial loaders, RayGlides conversion kits turn your standard EV into a solar-assisted hybrid cargo vehicle. Our app gives drivers real-time diagnostics, performance tracking, and direct financial metrics.

KEY FEATURES:
• Glow-charge Dial: Instantly monitor your Battery State of Charge (SOC) and State of Health (SOH) diagnostics to ensure your battery is operating within safe parameters.
• Live Solar Input: Track real-time solar power generation (in Watts and Voltages) flowing from the roof-mounted solar array directly into your battery.
• Cost Savings Calculator: Calculate exactly how much money you save daily! RayGlides translates your clean solar power generation (kWh) into saved commercial charging tariffs in real time.
• Live GPS Vehicle Tracker: Never lose sight of your loader. Real-time GPS location tracking shows your exact coordinates and diagnostic health metrics on an interactive map.
• Multi-factor Secure Login: Fast signup and secure login using standard password authentication or One-Time Passwords (OTP) generated on-demand. Compatible with Google Sign-in.

Drive cleaner, save more, and take full control of your EV fleet with RayGlides!
```

## 3. Graphic Assets Checklist

1. **App Icon**:
   - High-res icon: `512 x 512 pixels`
   - Format: `32-bit PNG`
   - Max file size: `1024 KB`
   - Theme: Green solar cell mesh with electric-blue circular charging line.

2. **Feature Graphic**:
   - Dimensions: `1024 x 500 pixels`
   - Format: `JPG` or `24-bit PNG`
   - Design: The high-graphics RayGlides 3-wheeler eco-loader (file: `rayglides_3w_loader.jpg`) centered with glowing green/blue circuit decals.

3. **Phone Screenshots** (Minimum 4):
   - Aspect ratio: `16:9` or `9:16`
   - Screen 1: Dashboard with glowing 78% SOC battery gauge.
   - Screen 2: Real-time map displaying driver location pin.
   - Screen 3: Savings view showing bar chart of weekly tariff earnings.
   - Screen 4: Login portal showing federated Google Sign-in.
